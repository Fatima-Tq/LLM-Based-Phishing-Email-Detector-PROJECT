ℹ️ How the System Works

🔍 Email Analysis Features

  • Sender Validation: Checks if sender domain is spoofed (e.g., paypel.com vs paypal.com)
  • Urgency Detection: Identifies urgent/threatening keywords ("Verify NOW", "Account Suspended", etc.)
  • Link Analysis: Extracts and analyzes all URLs in email
  • Link Suspicion: Flags shortened URLs, typosquatted domains, IP addresses
  • Body Length: Unusually short emails are more likely phishing

🔗 Link Checking Threats Detected

  • Phishing Domains: paypel.com, amaz0n.com, paypa1.com, goog1e.com, etc.
  • Typosquatting: Misspelled famous domains (mimics real company names)
  • IP Addresses: Direct IP instead of domain name (suspicious)
  • Shortened URLs: bit.ly, tinyurl, ow.ly (hides actual destination)
  • Suspicious TLDs: .tk, .ml, .ga (free domains used by attackers)
  • Unicode Domains: Homograph attacks using similar-looking characters

📊 Risk Score Levels

  • 0-30%: 🟢 Low Risk - Email appears legitimate
  • 31-70%: 🟡 Medium Risk - Suspicious characteristics detected
  • 71-100%: 🔴 High Risk - Likely phishing, do NOT click links
⚠️ Important Security Tips
  • Never click links in suspicious emails - verify directly with company
  • Check sender email carefully (paypel.com ≠ paypal.com)
  • Hover over links to see actual destination
  • Be wary of urgent language and account threats
  • Major companies never ask for passwords via email
